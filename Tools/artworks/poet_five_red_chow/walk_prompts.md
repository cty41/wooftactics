4. `frame 4 of 8` — head rises slightly; opposite diagonal pair begins lead; weapon absent.
5. `frame 5 of 8` — head neutral; torso level; right foreleg forward; left foreleg back; right hindleg back; left hindleg forward; weapon absent.
6. `frame 6 of 8` — head steady; torso slightly down; leading paws contact; trailing paws lift; weapon absent.
7. `frame 7 of 8` — head steady; torso lowest; legs pass beneath body; weapon absent.
